## Meaning to establish before Part I

W maps each cell's outgoing synaptic activation into every receiving cell's input. It is needed because the fate of a perturbation depends on who affects whom, the size and sign of each effect, and repeated feedback. It does not encode represented angle directly and does not by itself ensure a stable attractor. Distinguish fixed W, changing state s, rate response phi, and the later local dynamics matrix K.

Fisher information describes how well a brief spike sample distinguishes nearby represented values under an observation model. It is central because the same noisy spikes both provide evidence to a receiver and renew the recurrent memory. Explain the bound as “small information forces at least this much diffusion; large information permits better stability but does not ensure the circuit attains it.” State that the bound is model-specific and that Fisher is neither a number of bits nor an answer about widely separated ambiguous values. No review of elementary expected value, variance, standard deviation or density is needed.

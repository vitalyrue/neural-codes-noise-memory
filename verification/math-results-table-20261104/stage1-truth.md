## Findings requiring repair

For size s measured in length^d, s^(2/d) has length squared units, so beta has inverse length squared units in all d. The table's beta formula and the detailed size chapter are correct. Say that the numerical geometric factor changes with dimension while the units remain inverse distance squared.
2. **Wrong source equation in the multidimensional row.** The label 'diffusion tensor; SI S14' points to a scalar variance calculation. 3. **2012 equality attainment is missing.** The paper gives an entire main-text subsection to whether the information-diffusion lower bound can actually be reached. Add the general pointwise condition v_i = r_i'/(tau J r_i), and symmetric weights plus an exponential Poisson transfer function as a sufficient class. Keep the detailed chapter's valid counterexample to the paper's overstrong converse: exponential transfer is not generally necessary. This is a principal result, not an optional extension.

Main text p3 and SI S44, S52-S56 explicitly give it. A tensor diffusion row without the corresponding information constraint leaves an important generalization out.

Give the defensible local Gaussian statement V_total(T,L) >= 2DT + sqrt(2D/J), approached by ideal readout as L tends to infinity. At saturation it becomes 2D(T+tau). Do not copy the printed universal lower bound 2D(T+tau): 2DJ tau^2 >= 1 instead implies sqrt(2D/J) <= 2D tau. The detailed readout chapter already handles this correctly and distinguishes an algebraic source inconsistency from a published correction.
6. **2025 high-threshold count and gap distributions are missing.** Means and high-threshold mean counts do not state N approximately Poisson(lambda) or gap approximately exponential. These are central full-distribution comparisons in main Figs1D and 2E, main text p3/p5, and Methods S3.

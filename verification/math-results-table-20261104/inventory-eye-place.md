## Printed-source cautions the final table must preserve

1. Mainali SI S18 multiplies by mean size although its preceding moment formula requires division. Correct: `beta=[Gamma(1+d/2)/mu_s]^(2/d)`.
2. Mainali SI S23 loses `sqrt(2)` when solving S22 for **full width**. Correct high-threshold width: `2 sqrt(2) sigma sqrt(epsilon/q)`, leading in 1D to `beta=q²/(8 sigma²)`.
3. Mainali SI S25 has the wrong sign on the finite-height quadratic term: the ratio is `exp(−q epsilon−epsilon²/2)`, hence `exp(−u−u²/(2q²))` after `u=q epsilon`. The exponential limiting law survives this sign correction.
4. These are checks of the printed derivation, not evidence that simulation code or empirical fits share its errors. The source has stale equation cross-references too; use the actual displayed equations, not the nearby pointer numbers.
5. Ben-Shushan Eq. 1 has a rate/sensitivity normalization ambiguity. State the fixed-slope convention rather than declaring a proven typo. Supplement mentions earlier figure/equation numbers and 15K integrator neurons; final main Methods specify 30,000. Keep those comparison contexts separate.
6. The place 3D fitted `sigma=4.0` has an unclear distance-unit conversion against 10 cm bins. Avoid presenting it as a verified metre value.

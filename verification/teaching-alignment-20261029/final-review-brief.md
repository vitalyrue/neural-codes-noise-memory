# Current text acceptance review brief

This file specifies the review procedure, not expected findings. The coordinator will provide the frozen current packet and assigned chapter group after text integration. Do not begin on an earlier packet.

## Reader and scope

The reader knows undergraduate calculus, basic probability and linear algebra. The guide teaches the neuroscience and the additional probability, information, filtering and random-field ideas it needs. Read the actual source and relevant earlier teaching before deciding that a premise is missing. Preview, mention and glossary lookup do not automatically teach a prerequisite.

The scope is all reader-facing text in the assigned group, including prose, equations, typed tables, figure captions and labels, alt text, fold titles, source notes, chapter and Part openings, and sidecar summaries. The eye group also checks the landing page and project description. The place group includes glossary and references. Current figure pixel acceptance is explicitly deferred by the user. Do not approve artwork from its caption or unchanged file hash.

Do not edit manuscript, sidecars, artwork, rules or installed tools. Report a defect and the meaning that must be supplied, rather than writing the final replacement. The coordinator writes repairs and requests a real later check. Optional preferences do not block acceptance. Report justified retained passages as well as defects.

## Independence and stages

Start with the current manuscript and neutral packet, the audience and rules. Do not read prior review verdicts or proposed-edit reasons before your own initial reading. A diff can identify changed passages without serving as an answer key. Preserve actual call times and current input/rule hashes; never relabel an earlier review as current.

The coordinator requests three separate calls in order:

1. `truth_reasoning`: identify what the text actually claims and what a reader would infer. Check roles, definitions, assumptions, derivations, units, examples and source support. Test the strongest reasonable complete reading, including nearby limits. Check changed passages first and their connections. Reuse unchanged source calculations only after verifying their actual connection and limits; repetition of every numerical calculation is not itself a requirement.
2. `necessity_language`: test why each claim belongs here, simpler sufficient wording, exact terms, first teaching and definition emphasis. Read style diagnostics as prompts, not proof. Do not rename scientific objects or change citation conventions to satisfy a pattern. Check each proposed exception against its actual passage and rule.
3. `understanding_flow`: assess what each paragraph adds, why it appears here and how it connects. Record a purpose, connection, decision and reason for every current paragraph. Check concept prerequisites and transitions, then read the connected chapter path without filling gaps from expert knowledge.

Use the installed course-study-guide-builder and course-quality-gate contracts, pinned to Publisher 0.8.23. Each actual call needs a local request ID, reviewer identity, fresh or reviewer_continuation context, measured timezone-aware start/end, current input hash and review-contract hash. Each unit needs supported reader questions and answers with its full current unit/paragraph IDs. Findings stay unresolved until actually repaired and checked. Keep separate call files.

Across the three calls, fill each of the packet's 16 review fields once from actual reading, not repeated boilerplate. Structured fields need their required components: system setting/roles/flow/assumptions/verdict; alternatives/common problem/options/relationship/criteria/scope/verdict when applicable; essential reader task/dependencies/reasoning chain/unresolved; expert objection/kind/target/disposition/reason. A concrete wording judgment includes the phrase, plain paraphrase, actor, object, action/state, condition/consequence, verdict and retention or replacement reason. Units with math, tables or figures require a correctness objection, not merely a style objection.

Every extracted claim row needs its actual verdict and check. It is a selected inventory, not all possible claims. There are no reader code listings to execute. The coordinator may serialize your judgments into the official packet but must not invent missing answers.

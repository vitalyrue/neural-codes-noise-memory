# Neural Codes, Noise, and Memory: A Guide to Three Papers

Neural circuits represent quantities such as location and eye position while individual spikes fluctuate. This guide develops the background and central calculations of Burak and Fiete (2012), Ben-Shushan and colleagues (2022), and Mainali and colleagues (2025).

Begin with the reading path for paper copies, assumed knowledge, and the route through the mathematics. The guide assumes undergraduate calculus, basic probability, and linear algebra. Neuroscience, diffusion, information, filtering, and spatial random-field ideas are taught before the calculations that use them.

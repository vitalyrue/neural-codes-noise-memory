## Finding requiring repair

**PF-S1-01, low severity, PLACE-WIRING, lines 426 and 452–459:** the section starts with “centered spatial tuning” and later uses the strictly positive Gaussian profile $u_0(s)=R_0e^{-s^2/(2b^2)}$. In the covariance context, centered means a mean has been subtracted. The displayed Gaussian is not mean-subtracted on a finite environment. The covariance calculation itself does not need centered inputs: fixed profiles and independent zero-mean weights already give $E[h(x)]=0$. The strongest reading that retains the passage is that centered was meant to describe the summed input, or the location of the individual tuning profile; neither is what line 426 literally says, especially after the guide's definition of centered covariance in FOUNDATIONS-COVARIANCE. Proposed repair: use “fixed spatial tuning” at line 426, add that the expectation is over weights and zero-mean weights center $h$, and allow the positive Gaussian as the subsequent illustrative profile. If the author instead retains mean-subtracted $u_i$, the example needs that subtraction and the resulting finite-domain covariance terms. No change to the existing integral is needed with the simpler first repair. Source: P3SI Methods S2, S2–S8; the source itself mixes a simplifying zero-mean input premise with the Gaussian-profile example, so repeating it does not remove the mismatch.
